## Renderer Overview
Octane built its reputation on being one of the first fully GPU-based unbiased renderers, computing light transport the way physics describes it rather than approximating shortcuts. This purity gives images a natural believability that is hard to fake, which is why product and design work gravitates toward it.

The spectral rendering core simulates wavelengths of light, capturing subtle dispersion and color effects that RGB-only engines miss. Combined with a real-time interactive viewport, artists sculpt lighting and materials while watching a live, progressively refining image respond to every change.

Its node-based material and rendering graph offers deep control for those who want it, while sensible presets keep newcomers productive. The result is an engine that satisfies both the technical purist and the deadline-focused creative working on visual polish.

Start by installing the Octane plugin for your chosen host or launching the standalone edition, then confirm your GPU and drivers are supported. Activate your license and open the live viewer, which becomes your primary workspace for judging light, materials, and composition as they evolve.
